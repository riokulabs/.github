## Why we ask for this (plain language, not part of the agreement)

You keep the copyright in what you contribute. This agreement gives us a licence to use it, and to choose the licence it is published under, within the limits below. We ask for it so that we can:

- keep the default licence, the GNU GPL v3.0, and still make some parts MIT (for example a tool), or offer them under two licences;
- change a project's licence later, or hand a project to a new maintainer, without having to find every past contributor;
- give Keen Software House the licence its EULA asks for when a mod is uploaded to Steam Workshop or mod.io.

What it does **not** allow:

- **Never closed source.** We can only use open source or free software licences (or, for art and other media, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0), plus the licence a game platform requires to publish a mod. We will not license your contribution under proprietary terms.
- **It never becomes less available.** Your contribution always stays available under the licence the project used on the day you submitted it.
- **Mods stay free.** Keen's EULA does not allow selling mods, and this agreement does not change that. Mods stay free and non-commercial.

You sign once, by posting a comment on your first pull request. One signature covers every Rioku Labs project. Section 8 explains how.

### 8. How to sign

**8.1 Signing.** Post this comment, from Your own GitHub account, on a pull request to any Rioku Labs repository:

```
I have read the Rioku Labs CLA and I hereby sign it
```

A bot records the signature. One signature covers all Rioku Labs projects and all Your later Contributions under this version.

**8.2 Under the age of majority.** If You are under the age of majority where You live (18 in most places), a parent or legal guardian must also agree. They post this comment on the same pull request, from their own GitHub account, replacing `<login>` with Your GitHub username:

```

```

The bot does not check this comment. We record it by hand. Do not post a date of birth or any other personal details.
