# Rioku Labs Entity Contributor Licence Agreement


**Version 1.0, 20 October 2026.**
This is the version for a company or other organisation whose employees or contractors contribute. Individuals sign the [individual version](CLA.md).

## Why we ask for this (plain language, not part of the agreement)

The reasons and limits are the same as in the [individual version](CLA.md): the default licence stays the GNU GPL v3.0; the agreement lets us make parts MIT, offer two licences, change a licence later, hand a project to a new maintainer, and give Keen Software House the licence its EULA asks for when a mod is published. It never allows closed source, a contribution always stays available under the licence used on the day it was submitted, and mods stay free and non-commercial.

Your organisation keeps the copyright. The difference is who signs: someone allowed to bind the organisation signs this document once, and lists the people who contribute for it (Schedule A).

### 8. How to sign, and how to change Schedule A

1. Fill in Schedule A, sign it, and send a PDF copy to Us through the contact page on https://rioku.dev. We will confirm when it is recorded.
2. Each Designated Contributor also posts the usual signing comment on their first pull request, so the bot can match their GitHub account:

   ```
   I have read the Rioku Labs CLA and I hereby sign it
   ```

   For a Designated Contributor, that comment confirms they are contributing for You under this Agreement, and covers anything they contribute in which they, rather than You, hold rights.
3. To add or remove a Designated Contributor, send Us an updated Schedule A signed by an authorised person. Removing someone does not affect Contributions they already Submitted.
